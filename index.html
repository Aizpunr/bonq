Sliding window

  • Only the last 26 cups count (about a year, as Bonq runs every other week).
  • Of those, only your best 18 results are summed. The rest are dropped.

How it works

  • 1st Winner is the first player to clinch: they beat the full lobby. After they leave, the field is weaker, so 2nd to 5th earn fewer points.
  • The best-of system means missing a few cups doesn't destroy your ranking, but you can't coast on old results forever.
  • Theoretical max: 18 × 200 = 3600.

Tiers

  • Legend: 2600+ points
  • Pro: 1300+ points
  • Master: 800+ points
  • Gold: 200+ points

Records tab

The Records tab shows all-time stats (wins, placements, finalist appearances) across every cup, plus Finish %: the share of rounds a player finished out of the rounds they were in the lobby for. It's a career record, not a ranking.

Convergent Skill Rating

A Glicko-inspired skill estimate for Bonq Cup, using the same settings as the Kerki Comp skill rating. Each result pulls your rating toward a performance value based on your placement.

How it works

  • Every player starts at 1500 (unproven baseline).
  • Each cup result pulls your rating toward a performance value: winning 1st pulls toward 2100, finalist toward 1700, and so on.
  • Early results cause big jumps (high uncertainty). After several cups, your rating stabilizes and moves slowly.

Decay & uncertainty

  • Inactivity decay: miss a cup and your rating decays 2% toward 1500.
  • Surprise widening: if a result is far from your current rating, the system becomes less certain and allows bigger adjustments.
  • ±σ shows uncertainty. High σ means not enough data yet; low σ means the rating is settled.

Limitations

  • No lobby context: winning against 30 players counts the same as winning against 7.
  • Players with few cups have high uncertainty, so their rating may not reflect true skill yet.
